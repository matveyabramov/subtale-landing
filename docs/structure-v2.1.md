# SUBTALE — landing structure v2.1

Status: provisional wireframe, not final HPV/4U copy; SUBTALE is a proposed product, not a launched service.

| # | Block | Task |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Hero | Show who SUBTALE is for, its benefit and distinctive hypothesis immediately; clear CTA, product placeholder |
| 02 | Problem / blind spots | Show the difference between not knowing a word and not noticing a missed meaning; no immediate quiz |
| 03 | Introducing SUBTALE | Show the scene + context breakdown interface in a large mockup |
| 04 | Two modes | Manually mark a scene; hypothesized automatic selection of potentially missed nuance |
| 05 | How it works | Watch → collect moments → interpret → reveal and understand |
| 06 | One scene, two meanings | Show interpretation before explanation using a specific scene; no separate full quiz |
| 07 | Compare approaches | Compare typical translation, language-learning, and proposed SUBTALE workflows; do not misrepresent competitors |
| 08 | FAQ | Clarify viewing, compatibility, quality, and that this is a concept |
| 09 | Final CTA | One honest action, currently an internal link to the sample scene |

## Critical decisions
- No interactive quiz immediately after Hero.
- Comparison comes after demonstrating product mechanics.
- The independent “card mechanics” section is merged into the how-it-works scenario.
- Do not claim that ordinary competitors cannot explain context or save interpretations.
- Automated detection, platform integrations, and trust claims require validation.
- Keep placeholder scenes/interfaces and draft text; no final visual brand identity yet.
